InterviewIQ interviewer characters
==================================

The six characters (maya, david, elena, james, priya, arjun .glb) were generated
with MPFB (MakeHuman Plugin For Blender, https://static.makehumancommunity.org/mpfb.html)
using MakeHuman community asset packs, and rigged for the TalkingHead library
(https://github.com/met4citizen/TalkingHead, MIT) using its CC0 rig and weights.

Asset licences
- MakeHuman base mesh, system assets, skins01/02, suits01, hair01, eyebrows01,
  eyelashes01, visemes02, faceunits01: CC0
- maya.glb hairstyle "elvs_wavy_bob" by Elvaerwyn (MakeHuman community hair02 pack): CC-BY
  https://www.makehumancommunity.org/
- Skin normal map from "mindfront" skins (MakeHuman community, CC0)

Regenerate the cast with scripts/avatars/build_cast.py (see the header of that
file), then compress with:  gltf-transform resize … --width 1536 && gltf-transform webp …
